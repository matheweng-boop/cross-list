How these were picked

One goal per rung of the Automotive map ladder ({{ f.counts.A }} Sell now, {{ f.counts.B }} Qualify, {{ f.counts.C }} New variant, {{ f.counts.D }} Adjacent, {{ f.counts.gap }} Gap calls over {{ f.n_apps }} car applications and {{ f.n_diagrams }} car block diagrams), plus the reference-design anchors and the other markets. Within a goal:

  • Sell now: the functions with an automotive-grade part of ours in the most car applications; the pair of our functions that meets most often in the car diagrams (Cross-sell).
  • Reference designs: competitor parts we cross, most public reference designs first (the anchors on Cross-sell).
  • Qualify, New variant and Draw from other markets: the Top moves of the map, ranked by the Opportunity score at the step's distance (weights: {% for k, w in f.weights.items() %}{{ k }} {{ w }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}; see Methodology). A New variant needs a business case in Research & ideas; the table leaves out functions goals 3 and 4 already name.
  • Gaps: functions neither company sells, scored at the distance of our closest technology (Gaps & new technology); crowded markets score lower.
  • Horizon and effort follow the distance: {% for d, e in effort.items() %}{{ d }} {{ dist_label(d)|lower }} → {{ e[0]|lower }}, {{ e[1] }}{{ '; ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} (all est).

  • TAM is shown where the TAM estimates page has a figure for the function; most functions have none yet.
    • {% endif %}
