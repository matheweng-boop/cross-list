How the score works · technology {{ w.technology }}% · likeliness {{ w.likeliness }}% · diagrams {{ w.diagrams }}%{{ ' (your weights)' if prio_custom }}

Priority (0–100) = 100 × ({{ '%.2f'|format(w.technology / 100) }} × technology relation + {{ '%.2f'|format(w.likeliness / 100) }} × likeliness + {{ '%.2f'|format(w.diagrams / 100) }} × diagrams). Each of the three is between 0 and 1. Technology relation weighs most, so RF and power work comes first; general power such as an LDO stays listed (under “All”) but below it. The Opportunity score next to it (Quick win, New chip …) is a separate measure: demand, distance, architecture fit, competition and trend (method).

Technology relation: how close the function is to our RF and power strengths. “RF & power only” shows {{ prio.focus_from }} or more. “All” shows every item in three groups, each highest priority first: RF & power ({{ prio.focus_from }} or more), then general power and adjacent technology ({{ prio.outside_below }} to below {{ prio.focus_from }}), then outside our technology focus (below {{ prio.outside_below }}). So an LDO in many diagrams never sits above RF or power work, whatever the weights.
