3. Our estimates (est)

  • 48 V net ranges (about 36–52 V normal, overvoltage near 60 V, transients to about 70 V): as commonly quoted from VDA 320 / LV 148; the standards were not read.
  • Actuator power ranges and safety levels in the first table: typical values from the vendor application lists (1.3, 1.4, 1.5) and our judgement; no OEM document read.
  • Current at 12 V vs 48 V: P / V (1 kW / 12 V ≈ 83 A, / 48 V ≈ 21 A).
  • Qualification path and the 105 °C limit of PAC5527 for Grade 1: from the PAC5527 review (datasheet Rev 1.2, distributor-hosted copy) and the AEC-Q100 grade definitions.
  • A 48 V-only 100 V-class die as a smaller, cheaper option: our judgement from the Elmos one-chip part.
  • Data-centre 48 V fans and pumps: from the 48 V rack bus in the Systems library (48 V intermediate bus converter diagram).
