4. Our estimates (est)

  • Vehicles built: 96.4 M of all types in 2025 (OICA, as recorded in the BizDev TAM topics; med).
  • Dedicated audio DSP sockets: 30–50 % of cars have audio processing outside the cockpit SoC or the tuner (external amplifiers in mid and premium trims, head units with their own audio DSP), so about 30–50 M a year. Our reading; no public split was read (low).
  • Price: $3.5–5.5 (one core), $6–9 (two cores), $9–13 (three cores) at contract volume, from TI's 1k list prices of $25–33 for larger parts with Arm cores less 30–50 % to contract and a smaller die (low). Average $6–10, so a market of about $0.2–0.5 B a year.
  • Hunting scores: fit, size and access each 1–3 from the facts above, multiplied (27 at most). Use-case needs (channels, latency, interfaces) are general knowledge and the wireless car audio report's loop budget.
  • Scenarios: 3 M one-core, 1.5 M two-core, 0.5 M three-core and 1 M head-unit sets a year, about 10–15 % of the sockets; not a forecast.
