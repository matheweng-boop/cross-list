4. Our estimates (est)

  • About 27 W per port from a 12 V battery with a buck alone: the output must stay below the lowest running input; 9 V × 3 A, a cold crank cuts it further.
  • 12 V battery from about 6 V in a cold crank to 16 V and more in normal running: typical figures; the profile is the carmaker's.
  • Effort marks (short, medium, long) follow the path data; “long” for our own PD stack and a high-voltage BMS AFE with ASIL D is our judgement.
  • A standalone automotive PD controller's price is low and falling: from the number of suppliers and consumer listings; no automotive price was read.
  • 48 V packs about 13–14 lithium cells, 12 V about 4: common chemistry figures.

  • Reuse marks (high / medium / low / none / new) are our judgement from the catalogue descriptions above; no Qorvo or Skyworks design data was used.
  • The CC line runs at about 300 kbit/s with a loose clock tolerance, so an on-chip oscillator is enough: from the USB PD specification as commonly implemented; the specification itself was not read in this run.
