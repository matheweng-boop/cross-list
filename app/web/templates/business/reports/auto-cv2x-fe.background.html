Regulation, spectrum and standards

  • US C-V2X band 5895–5925 MHz in three 10 MHz channels; industry uses 20 MHz (5905–5925) first, then 10 MHz (5895–5905), which "would require a second C-V2X radio"; DSRC must stop by 14 Dec 2026; geofenced higher OBU power, certification process not yet defined; NR-V2X direct not applicable in North America · 5.9 GHz Second Report and Order – FAQ, 5GAA, Feb 2025 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2025/02/faq-5.9-ghz.pdf
  • UNII-4 Wi-Fi 5850–5895 MHz directly below the C-V2X channels · FCC First Report and Order (2020), from general knowledge, not re-read · med
  • EU: 5855–5875 MHz non-safety, 5875–5925 MHz safety ITS (rail to 5935); 10–20 MHz for day-1 services, whole 70–75 MHz needed; 20 MHz channels need a rule change (update, Nov 2026: CEPT conclusions now enable "wider 20 MHz channels for road-safety ITS", and the revision of the EU 5.9 GHz Decision is "expected to start in 2026" · 5GAA Annual Report 2025, p. 20); ITS-G5 stays on 5895–5905 MHz for years; ITS-G5 to add 802.11bd · 5.9 GHz band configuration for road-ITS deployment in Europe, 5GAA position paper, Mar 2024 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2024/03/5gaa-position-paper-european-road-its
  • 5G-V2X Direct market readiness 2026–2029; European OEMs explicitly back it; first OEM-to-OEM demo Oct 2024 · 5GAA Annual Report 2024, 2025 · med (roadmap figure) — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2025/05/5gaa-annual-report-web.pdf
  • Rel-18 sidelink evolution: sidelink carrier aggregation and sidelink in unlicensed n46/n96/n102 · An Overview of 5G Advanced Evolution in 3GPP Release 18, arXiv · med (snippet) — arxiv.org/pdf/2201.01358
  • USDOT plan: short term (to 2028) at least 2 OEMs commit to 5.9 GHz by MY2028; medium term (2029–2031) 5 capable vehicle models, 4 suppliers; long term (2032–2036) at least 6 OEMs; update, Nov 2026: the plan was "paused in 2025 due to changes in administration" (5GAA Annual Report 2025, p. 21) · Saving Lives with Connectivity: Accelerate V2X Deployment (summary), USDOT, Aug 2024 · low (search snippets; the PDF URL returned an HTML page) — its.dot.gov/research_areas/emerging_tech/pdf/Accelerate_V2X_Deployment

  • BAW filters preferred for C-V2X coexistence because they cover the ITS band; newer parts extend filtering into 6–7 GHz · Wireless coexistence challenges in C-V2X, Electronics360 · low (snippet; fetch returned 403) — electronics360.globalspec.com/article/17398/wireless-coexistence-chall
  • 5GAA background: deployment status 2025. Roadmap figure of expected mass-deployment timelines 2020–2030 tied to 3GPP Rel-16 to Rel-20, roadmap update due 2026 (p. 6); EU: 20 MHz channels enabled by CEPT, 5.9 GHz Decision revision from 2026, "lack of harmonised progress on V2X Direct", Dual-Technology study of adjacent channels (p. 20); US: Atlanta first Day One Deployment District, Aug 2025; USDOT plan paused in 2025 (p. 21); first on-road 5G-V2X Direct demo, Valeo with Marben, Paris, May 2025 (p. 27); China GB/T 45315-2025 for LTE-V2X Direct, Korea ≈50 km LTE-V2X corridor and one K-NCAP point, Japan and India 5.9 GHz allocations (pp. 18–19); China NCAP 2027 explores C-V2X-triggered warnings, Europe's 2029 NCAP roadmap considers V2X (p. 8). Our reading (est): more regions on one band favour one wide-band two-path front end · 5GAA Annual Report 2025, 5GAA, Apr 2026 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2026/04/5gaa-annual-report-2025.pdf
  • 5GAA background: China. First national LTE-V2X direct OBU standard published, with BSM and OBU component requirements and test methods; follow-on standard for direct-link safety warnings started (p. 13); vehicle-road-cloud pilot 2024–2026 in 20 cities (pp. 10, 24); pilot cities deploy LTE-V2X direct RSUs and new L2+ cars there "are encouraged to carry C-V2X terminals" (p. 25); C-NCAP 2024 test cases targeted for C-V2X (p. 17). Our reading (est): fitment stays policy-led; no NR-V2X direct date given · V2X State of Play in China II (TR v1.5), 5GAA, Feb 2025 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2025/02/5gaa-tr-wi-v2x-china-sop-ii-v1.5-clea
  • 5GAA background: spectrum needs. Day-1 LTE-V2X needs "between 10 and 20 MHz" at 5.9 GHz; advanced LTE-V2X + NR-V2X "an additional 40 MHz or more" (pp. 6, 52); at least 20 MHz recommended for day-1 messages (p. 33); the whole "70-75 MHz of ITS spectrum in the 5.9 GHz band" is needed (pp. 7, 52); allocations must exceed the minimum or collisions rise near 100 % load (p. 52); V2N needs ≥50 MHz more low-band (< 1 GHz) and ≥500 MHz more mid-band (pp. 7, 53). Our reading (est): several 10–20 MHz carriers at once support a wide-band two-path front end · Updated study of spectrum needs for safety-related ITS, day 1 and advanced use cases V2.0, 5GAA, Oct 2021 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2021/10/5GAA_Day1_and_adv_Use_Cases_Spectrum_
  • 5GAA background: EU coexistence with ITS-G5 (802.11p). Same-channel use gives "mutually harmful co-channel interference" (p. 2); step 1 one preferred 10 MHz channel each in 5875–5905 MHz (e.g. 5875–5885 and 5895–5905 MHz); steps 2–3 mutual detect-and-vacate in the middle channel, then all channels; full 5855–5925 MHz sharing later via EN 302 571 (pp. 1–5). Our reading (est): refined since by ETSI TR 103 766 and the 2024 band plan; still why EU radios must tune all channels and detect the other waveform · Coexistence of C-V2X and ITS-G5 at 5.9 GHz, position paper Rev-1, 5GAA, Apr 2018 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2018/10/Position-Paper-ITG5.pdf
  • 5GAA background: EU radio profile for C-V2X (PC5) Day 1. Access layer per ETSI TS 103 613 (p. 8); a C-ITS sub-band under EN 302 571 for all messages and transmit power control per ETSI TS 103 574 (p. 10); in tolling zones power set to Pregulatory,c per 3GPP TS 36.101 except top-priority DENMs (p. 9); ITS-G5 DCC sensitivity parameters do not apply (p. 10). Our reading (est): no new RF numbers; the front end needs fine power control and a tolling-zone power step · Initial C-V2X System Profile, TR S-180179 v1.1, 5GAA, 2018 (published Feb 2020) · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2020/02/5GAA_S-180179_TR_ICSP_Initial_C-V2X_S
