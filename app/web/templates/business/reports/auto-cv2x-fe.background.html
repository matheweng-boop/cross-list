Regulation, spectrum and standards

  • US C-V2X band 5895–5925 MHz in three 10 MHz channels; industry uses 20 MHz (5905–5925) first, then 10 MHz (5895–5905), which "would require a second C-V2X radio"; DSRC must stop by 14 Dec 2026; geofenced higher OBU power, certification process not yet defined; NR-V2X direct not applicable in North America · 5.9 GHz Second Report and Order – FAQ, 5GAA, Feb 2025 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2025/02/faq-5.9-ghz.pdf
  • UNII-4 Wi-Fi 5850–5895 MHz directly below the C-V2X channels · FCC First Report and Order (2020), from general knowledge, not re-read · med
  • EU: 5855–5875 MHz non-safety, 5875–5925 MHz safety ITS (rail to 5935); 10–20 MHz for day-1 services, whole 70–75 MHz needed; 20 MHz channels need a rule change; ITS-G5 stays on 5895–5905 MHz for years; ITS-G5 to add 802.11bd · 5.9 GHz band configuration for road-ITS deployment in Europe, 5GAA position paper, Mar 2024 · high — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2024/03/5gaa-position-paper-european-road-its
  • 5G-V2X Direct market readiness 2026–2029; European OEMs explicitly back it; first OEM-to-OEM demo Oct 2024 · 5GAA Annual Report 2024, 2025 · med (roadmap figure) — 5gaa.org/content/uploads/2025/05/5gaa-annual-report-web.pdf
  • Rel-18 sidelink evolution: sidelink carrier aggregation and sidelink in unlicensed n46/n96/n102 · An Overview of 5G Advanced Evolution in 3GPP Release 18, arXiv · med (snippet) — arxiv.org/pdf/2201.01358
  • USDOT plan: short term (to 2028) at least 2 OEMs commit to 5.9 GHz by MY2028; medium term (2029–2031) 5 capable vehicle models, 4 suppliers; long term (2032–2036) at least 6 OEMs · Saving Lives with Connectivity: Accelerate V2X Deployment (summary), USDOT, Aug 2024 · low (search snippets; the PDF URL returned an HTML page) — its.dot.gov/research_areas/emerging_tech/pdf/Accelerate_V2X_Deployment
  • Wi-Fi 6E named as an additional interferer to C-V2X; I2V more robust than V2I under adjacent-channel interference · 5.9GHz Interference Resiliency for Connected Vehicle Equipment, USDOT/ROSAP · low (snippet; host blocked) — rosap.ntl.bts.gov/view/dot/72842
