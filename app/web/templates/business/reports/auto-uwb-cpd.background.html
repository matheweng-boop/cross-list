Requirements

  • Points (same document): Occupant Monitoring 30; Occupant Presence 10 = CPD 5 + crash occupancy 5; child left behind: warning 1.5 (rear only) / 3.0 (all passenger seats), warning + intervention 3.0 / 4.0; child entering unlocked vehicle 0.5 / 1.0; rear seat occupancy 5 points · high
  • Timing and coverage (same document): warning ≤ 15 s after locking; ≤ 10 min after door closure for a child who entered an unlocked car; initial warning ≥ 3 s; all seat rows, movable and removable seats; driver seat and all footwells for unlocked entry; luggage area excluded · high
