3. Our estimates (est)

  • Bluetooth Classic audio latency about 100–200 ms and LE Audio about 20–40 ms: typical values for the codec and buffers, not measured in a car.
  • Lip-sync with a screen needs tens of milliseconds; noise cancellation, chimes and main speakers stay wired: from 2.1–2.3 and 2.12.
  • Headphones are accessories, so consumer-grade parts fit; the hub and any speaker fixed in the car need automotive grade.
  • An Auracast broadcast code keeps a car's stream to its passengers (from the LE Audio specification set, not read for this report).
  • Buses, motorcycles and in-cabin gaming as further fields: from 1.3 and 1.5.
