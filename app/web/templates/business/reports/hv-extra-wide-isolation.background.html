1. Creepage and clearance the barrier needs

  • 1.1 · IEC 60664-1 creepage, basic insulation, material group I, pollution degree 2: 4.0 mm at 800 V, 5.0 mm at 1000 V, 6.3 mm at 1250 V, 8.0 mm at 1600 V; PD3: 10, 12.5, 16 and 20 mm. Reinforced = 2 × basic. So 800 V needs 8.0 mm, 1000 V 10 mm and 1500 V about 15 mm reinforced at PD2 (1500 V interpolated between 1250 and 1600 V, est) · MINMAX Technology, “Clearance and Creepage Distance in PCB Design”, read 21 Oct 2026 (cached from the creepage research); TI SLUP419 / SLUP421 (Mar 2024) give the same values · high — minmaxpower.com, ti.com/lit/pdf/slup419
  • 1.2 · 1500 V DC solar inverter: the customer's TÜV assessor said 15 mm creepage and clearance “is a must” for reinforced isolation in a 1500 V system (PCB material group I); TI answered that it has no device with 15 mm and that ISO7821DWW (14.5 mm) may be judged short of the IEC 62109-1 requirement; DWW is rated 1000 Vrms (EN 61010-1) and 1450 Vrms (EN 60950-1) working voltage, DW 600 / 800 Vrms · “ISO7821: 1500v solar inverter system”, TI E2E isolation forum, 7–8 Sep 2020, read 21 Oct 2026 · med (vendor forum, TI engineer's answer) — e2e.ti.com/…/938435
  • 1.3 · Skyworks package table: WB SSO-8 >8.5 mm creepage and >9.5 mm clearance (measured); WB SOIC-16 >7.6–8 mm; upcoming ultra-wide SOIC >15.0 mm creepage, >15.5 mm clearance (Table 6). 800 V DC reinforced at PD2: 8 mm on the package, 16 mm on FR4 (group IIIa), hence board slots (≥1 mm, 2 mm at PD3) or Type 2 coating. Altitude factor for clearance 1.14 at 3000 m, 1.48 at 5000 m; 600 V (400/690 V) OVC III reinforced clearance 8.0 mm at 2000 m, 9.12 mm at 3000 m, 11.84 mm at 5000 m: wide body “No”, ultra-wide “Yes” (Table 4). Data centers: PD2; zones OVC IV (outdoor, 1500 V DC renewables), OVC III (inverters, SST cells, central 800 V / 1500 V DC links, ESS controllers), OVC II (power shelves, BBUs, sidecars); Open Rack v3 asks for 3000 m. SST cells: gate drivers only need functional insulation with a grounded midpoint; the safety barrier between cells uses fiber optics · Skyworks AN1456 (207474B), “Understanding Creepage and Clearance Requirements for Data Centers”, 8 Sep 2026 (BizDev app-note cache) · high — skyworksinc.com/…/AN1456
  • 1.4 · PV moves from 1000 V to 1500 V DC (IEC 62109-2); 690 V AC drives (IEC 61800-5-1); systems above 1000 Vrms working, 8000 V impulse, 2000 m altitude or with higher pollution “may require clearance and creepage distances greater than 9 mm”; most reinforced isolated amplifiers are under 9 mm; AMC1411 / AMC1400 in stretched SOIC (DWL): ≥14.7 mm clearance, ≥15.7 mm creepage, 10.6 kVpk, 7.5 kVrms, 100 kV/µs · TI SBAA527, “TI's First Isolated Amplifiers With Ultra-Wide Creepage and Clearance”, Aug 2021, read 21 Oct 2026 · high — ti.com/lit/ab/sbaa527
  • 1.5 · Solar inverter environment: pollution degree 3 outside, 2 inside the IP65 enclosure · TÜV SÜD IEC 62109-1 test report for Sungrow SH3.0–6.0RS, May 2021 (cached from the creepage research) · high
  • 1.6 · Rail: a designer of a tram auxiliary converter (600 V DC line, EN 50124, PD3, OV3) computes 18.1 mm (material group I) to 20.1 mm (group II) reinforced creepage; ADuM4146 on Wolfspeed's evaluation driver has 8.3 mm and the DC-DC module 2 mm. Wolfspeed: its drivers follow UL / IEC 61800-5-1 and are for the lab; for very high isolation “some designers use fiber optic isolation such as the Broadcom AFBR-2634Z or use a larger footprint isolators such as the Texas Instruments ISO7841F” · “HAS310M17BM3 gate driver for Railway application”, Wolfspeed Power Applications Forum, Dec 2025, read 21 Oct 2026 · med (forum; the 18–20 mm is the poster's reading of EN 50124-1) — forum.wolfspeed.com/discussion/comment/3417
  • 1.7 · Not qualifying (our reading, est): inside the car the high-voltage parts sit in sealed housings (PD2), so 8 mm covers 800 V reinforced; a full 800 V battery (~900 V) would need 9 mm (ISO7821DWW report, section 4). Heat pumps and HVAC: 230/400 V mains, 400–800 V DC bus, indoor or sealed outdoor units, 4–8 mm at PD2. Medical: two means of patient protection (2 MOPP) at 250 Vrms is commonly quoted as 8 mm creepage (IEC 60601-1; standard not read). Electrified off-road machines: 400–800 V in sealed housings (PD2); PD3 only on open boards. Hydrogen electrolyser rectifiers: 1000–1500 V DC like solar and storage; no public source read · low (our inference)
