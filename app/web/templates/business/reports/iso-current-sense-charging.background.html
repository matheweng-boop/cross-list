Integrated isolated current sensing for charging: background notes

Every figure with its source and confidence (high = primary document read; med = trade press, forum or advocacy body; low = search snippet or analyst press release). “est” = our estimate, with its basis. Asked by the owner in the 23 Oct review: “Current sensing is big and more important in charging applications. Power isolation plays a role for EV charging. So low error and fully integrated could be the key here. Interesting to know if there is a market for shunt + DC-DC built in, or others?”

1. Is there a market for “shunt + DC-DC built in”?

  • 1.1 · DC-DC built in: yes, proven. TI's AMC3301 is a ±250 mV reinforced isolated amplifier “with integrated DC/DC”: a 3.3 or 5 V single supply on the low-voltage side; offset ±150 µV max, offset drift ±1 µV/°C max, gain error ±0.2 % max, gain drift ±40 ppm/°C max; “the fully integrated, isolated DC/DC converter allows single-supply operation from the low-side of the device”. TI lists AMC3311 (voltage), AMC3302-Q1, AMC3306M05-Q1, AMC3330 and AMC3336 in the same family · TI product page AMC3301, read 23 Oct 2026; BizDev parts data (data/real/ti_isolated_amplifiers.yaml) · high — ti.com/product/AMC3301
  • 1.2 · TI uses the built-in-DC/DC parts in its own 800 V charger designs: TIDA-01606 (11 kW three-level T-type inverter / PFC, 800 V, for DC fast charging and V2G) uses TMCS1123 and AMC3306M05; TIDA-010210 (11 kW ANPC, DC bus up to 1000 V) uses AMC3302 with UCC14130-Q1 isolated bias for the drivers; TIDA-010054 (10 kW DAB) uses AMC1306M05 and AMC1302 with an external supply · TI tool pages, read 21 Oct 2026 for the extra-wide isolation case (its background 2.1–2.3) · high — TIDA-01606, TIDA-010210, TIDA-010054
  • 1.3 · Shunt (conductor) built in: yes, but as Hall sensors and only for smaller currents. TI TMCS1123: galvanically isolated Hall-effect sensor, the current flows through an internal conductor; 80 A rms continuous (measurable range up to ±96 A); “less than 1.4 % maximum sensitivity error over temperature and lifetime with no system level calibration, or less than 0.9 % ... with a one-time calibration at room temperature”; sensitivity error ±0.1 %, offset ±0.2 mV, non-linearity ±0.1 %; 250 kHz, 1 µs response, 100 ns over-current detection; 5 kVrms, reinforced, 600 Vrms working, 8.1 mm creepage and clearance; 3–5.5 V single supply · TI product page TMCS1123, read 23 Oct 2026 · high — ti.com/product/TMCS1123
  • 1.4 · An isolated amplifier with a shunt inside the package: none in the BizDev parts data (Skyworks, TI, Infineon, Novosense, Chipanalog isolated amplifiers) and none on the pages read. TI's in-package-shunt current monitors (EZShunt, INA7xx) are not isolated (known from earlier reading, not re-read; low). A built-in shunt therefore competes with the integrated Hall sensors of 1.3 below about 80–100 A (est) · BizDev data; our reading
  • 1.5 · Other ways to integrate (our reading, est): Hall with in-package conductor (Allegro, Melexis, Infineon, TI), closed-loop or fluxgate modules (LEM and others) for 50–1000 A and for billing-grade DC metering in fast chargers, shunt modules with their own isolated electronics for battery current (e.g. Isabellenhütte). None of these were read in this run · low

2. Accuracy and error budget

  • 2.1 · Skyworks AN1270 (Oct 2023): static error of Si89xx = offset + gain error + nonlinearity, each over –40 to 125 °C; example Si8922B: offset –0.218 / +0.260 % FS, gain error –0.440 / +0.356 %, nonlinearity ±0.200 %; total by direct sum –0.858 / +0.816 %, by root-sum-of-squares (the method AN1270 suggests) –0.530 / +0.484 % · Skyworks AN1270 “Estimating Total Static Error for Skyworks Si89xx Isolated Amplifiers and Delta-Sigma Modulators” (BizDev app-note cache) · high
  • 2.2 · Datasheet values (BizDev parts data): Si8921BD-AS4 (SSO-8, 5 kVrms reinforced, automotive): ±250 mV, gain error 0.2 %, offset 150 µV, nonlinearity 0.02 %, 600 kHz, SNR 82 dB, CMTI 50 kV/µs, 6 / 4.5 mA supplies. Si8941BD (modulator): gain error 0.18 %, offset 180 µV, nonlinearity 0.004 %, SNR 90 dB, CMTI 50 kV/µs. TI AMC3301 (built-in DC/DC): 4.25 kVrms, gain error 0.2 %, offset 150 µV, nonlinearity 0.04 %, 334 kHz, SNR 71 dB, CMTI 85 kV/µs. AMC3302-Q1: ±50 mV, 0.2 %, 50 µV, 0.03 %, 340 kHz, 70 dB, 85 kV/µs. AMC1302-Q1 (external supply): 5 kVrms, 0.2 %, 50 µV, 280 kHz, 70 dB, 55 kV/µs · high (data/real/skyworks_isolated_amplifiers.yaml, data/real/ti_isolated_amplifiers.yaml)
  • 2.3 · The shunt adds its own error (est): a ±0.5 % shunt with 50 ppm/K over a 100 K rise adds about ±0.5 % + ±0.5 %; RSS with the amplifier's ±0.5 % gives about ±0.9–1.0 % uncalibrated, and about ±0.5 % after one gain calibration at end of line. A ±1 % shunt makes it about ±1.3 % (est). So an uncalibrated shunt chain is close to an integrated Hall sensor (±1.4 %, 1.3); the shunt wins after calibration, at high current and on offset, noise and bandwidth · low (est from typical shunt data, not read)
  • 2.4 · Power for the high side: Si8921 draws about 6 mA at 3–5.5 V on the input side (about 30 mW at 5 V); Si86Px gives up to 0.5 W isolated at 43 % peak efficiency from an integrated DC-DC and miniature transformer (3.3 / 5 V in, 3.3 / 5 V out) · Si86Px datasheet (BizDev cache); BizDev parts data · high
  • 2.5 · Si86Px today: dual and quad digital isolators with integrated power, SOIC-16W, 3.75 kVrms basic, industrial, samples only (release Mar 2026) · BizDev parts data (data/real/skyworks_si86.yaml) · high

3. Market

  • 3.1 · Current sensor market: USD 3.35 billion in 2026 to 6.39 billion by 2032, 11.4 % a year; drivers EVs, battery-powered systems, renewables, automation; “isolated current sensors hold the largest market share”, used in EV powertrains, BMS, charging infrastructure, motor drives and inverters; contact-based sensing is the largest sensing method and industrial the second-fastest-growing application; split by method, loop type, offering, technology, mounting, range, output, application and region, but the press release gives no automotive or charging share; key companies Asahi Kasei, Infineon, Allegro, LEM, Melexis, TDK, Honeywell, TI, Tamura, Omron, ST, Yageo, ADI, Broadcom, Aceinna · MarketsandMarkets press release “Current Sensor Industry worth $6.39 billion by 2032”, read 23 Oct 2026 (published 29 Sep 2026, report code SE 6985; paid report not read) · low — marketsandmarkets.com
  • 3.2 · DC fast chargers: about 2 million public fast chargers in 2024; ultra-fast (≥150 kW) +50 % · IEA Global EV Outlook 2025 (search snippet recorded in the extra-wide isolation case, 3.5) · low
  • 3.3 · Sense points per charger (est): an 11 kW OBC has 2–3 PFC phase currents, DC-link voltage, output current and battery voltage: 3–5 isolated amplifiers; a 30 kW DC fast-charger module 3–4 (extra-wide isolation case, ranked table); TI's 11 kW references use 2–4 · low (est from TIDA-01606, TIDA-010210 and the library diagrams)
  • 3.4 · Volume scenarios in the case are our assumptions, not forecasts: 0.5 M chargers × 3 sense points; 0.5 M parts in fast chargers and storage; 1 M built-in-shunt parts · low (est)
