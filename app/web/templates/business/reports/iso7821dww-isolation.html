Reinforced digital isolators for power, charging and cars: TI ISO7821DWW and our 8 mm limit

21 Oct 2026 · Research idea for a business case · built only from BizDev data: parts database (TI, Skyworks, Novosense digital isolators), cross results, block-diagram library (219 application diagrams), TI reference designs and the library topic on traction-inverter isolation. No new web research; open points are marked “open”.

In brief

  1. The part: TI ISO7821DWW is a 2-channel (one each way) reinforced digital isolator in a 16-pin extra-wide SOIC (DWW): 5.7 kVrms, 2828 Vpk working voltage, 14.5 mm creepage, 100 Mbps, 85 kV/µs CMTI, –55 to 125 °C, industrial grade, enable pins on both sides.
  2. Our position: our closest crosses, Si86S621 and Si86S622 (SSO-8), are reinforced too and better on CMTI (100 kV/µs), isolation rating (6 kVrms) and speed (150 Mbps), with automotive (-AS4) versions. But none of our 672 digital isolators passes 8 mm creepage; TI has 20 parts and Novosense 6 at 14.5–15 mm.
  3. Where it matters: digital isolators sit in 66 of the 219 application diagrams, among them the HEV/EV on-board charger, DC fast charger, AC wallboxes, the HV-to-LV DC/DC and traction inverters, next to our isolated gate drivers, isolated DC-DC and isolated amplifiers.
  4. The business-case question: whether 800 V chargers and inverters push customers past 8 mm, and whether an extra-wide package for our existing isolator die (plus the neighbours) is worth it. Market size is open.

What could combine into a product, strongest first

  1. Offer Si86S621/622-AS4 where 8 mm is enough. Better CMTI, kVrms and speed, automotive grade, today. Different package and pinout (8 vs 16 pins): a board change, not a drop-in.
  2. An extra-wide-package version of our 2-channel isolator. Same die in a 14.5 mm-class package to meet the creepage TI and Novosense offer. Open: package availability, cost and qualification time.
  3. An isolation set for the charger and inverter: isolator + Si829x / Si8285/86 gate drivers + Si89xx sensing + isolated bias, sold as one reference. Open: a reinforced isolated bias controller is missing.

2. Where else we see opportunities

  • Charging: HEV/EV on-board charger (two-stage PFC + isolated DC/DC), DC fast charging station, AC wallboxes (TI, NXP and Renesas diagrams) and wireless EV charging; each also uses our gate drivers, isolated DC-DC or isolated amplifiers.
  • Car power: traction inverters (400 / 800 V), HV-to-LV DC/DC, HV battery system and power distribution box, e-compressor, vehicle control unit.
  • Power conversion: solar string, micro and central inverters, energy-storage PCS, UPS, server and data-center battery backup, portable power stations.
  • Reference designs: TI TIDA-00785 (isolated GaN driver) pairs ISO7821 with a GaN gate driver and an isolated supply: the same pairing we can offer.

3. Market trend

  • Crowded and price-driven: TI, ADI, Infineon, ST, onsemi, Novosense, Chipanalog, 2Pai, Toshiba and Rohm; “established leaders plus fast-growing Chinese suppliers; increasingly price-competitive”.
  • Higher voltage: 800 V SiC is now mainstream in traction (library topic), with CMTI of 100–200 kV/µs and reinforced isolation; creepage and working-voltage needs rise with it. Open: the required creepage per IEC 60664-1 / ISO 6469-3 is not verified here.
  • Wide packages as a differentiator: in BizDev only TI (20 parts) and Novosense (6, e.g. NSI8321W1 at 15 mm and 7.5 kVrms) reach 14.5–15 mm.
  • Open: market size, volumes and prices for extra-wide isolators are not in BizDev.

Risks

  • Creepage above 8 mm may be needed only in a few designs; most chargers may be fine with 8 mm (open).
  • A new package means qualification time and cost against a price-driven market.
  • Our working voltage (2121 Vpk) is below ISO7821DWW's (2828 Vpk), and our low-temperature end is –40 °C vs –55 °C.

Open questions

  • Which customers asked for ISO7821DWW, in which product, and at what volume?
  • What creepage and working voltage do 800 V OEMs require for the charger and inverter barrier?
  • Can our 2-channel isolator die go into an extra-wide package, and what would it cost?
  • What do TI and Novosense charge for their extra-wide parts?
