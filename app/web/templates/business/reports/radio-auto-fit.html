In brief

  1. The radio core is automotive already. All Si479xx tuners (24 of 25), all Si469xx and Si461x coprocessors and the Si462x receivers are AEC-Q100; the Si469xx run from −40 to 105 °C.
  2. (A) Automotive today, 11 part groups: besides the radio core, SKYA21001 and QPC8010Q switches that reach the FM band, QPL6202Q/6208Q/6220Q satellite-radio (SDARS) LNAs, Si5335x-AM clock buffers, the ACT4751 2 MHz buck and the SKY5A2110 GNSS LNA.
  3. (B) Would need improvements, 6: Si473x and Si470x consumer tuners (−20 °C, one tuner), QPL7433/7442 wideband LNAs (no automotive amplifier design), the SKY76206 audio amplifier (phone class), SKY8700x bucks (WLCSP, last time buy) and the ACT4529 buck (125 kHz, harmonics in the AM band).
  4. (C) Only lacks qualification, 6: Si468x digital radio receivers (−40 to 85 °C, same functions as the AEC-Q100 Si462x), Si47992 (grade not in our data), AS169/AS183 and SKY13374 switches (300 kHz up, so they also cover AM), Si510/511 oscillators (−40 to 85 °C), ACT8832x PMIC (Not enough data on temperature).
  5. Gaps, 9 blocks: TVS/ESD, reverse polarity, LDO, supervisor, audio codec, CAN, automotive Ethernet, A²B and the active antenna amplifier. Flash, the head-unit SoC and the antenna are the customer's parts.

6. Where else we see opportunities

  • Remote tuner and roof modules: the radio moves to the antenna in zonal cars; a kit of tuner, switch, oscillator and 2 MHz buck is the step before the radio node of the case radio-tuner-next. Grade 2 (105 °C) matters there (est).
  • US satellite radio antennas: QPL62xxQ LNAs are automotive already; pairing them with our tuners and the GNSS module gives one antenna supplier for SDARS, GNSS and FM (est).
  • Head-unit clocks: the Si5332-AM clock generators (automotive) serve the head-unit SoC, PCIe and Ethernet; see auto-clock-tree.
  • Two-wheelers, trucks and aftermarket radios: the place for a Si473x with a −40 °C grade (est).

For the decision: The same short list sells into remote tuner and roof modules, where the radio is heading.

7. Market trend

  • Volume: about 90–92 M car radio systems a year, flat (owner, 25 Oct 2026); car radio content $0.89–2.31 B (2024) in the site's TAM band.
  • Digital radio rules: EU new cars must receive digital radio (EECC, from Dec 2020); HD Radio in more than 60 % of new US cars; DRM in 1 in 3 Indian cars; China's CDR rule drafted, not adopted (sources of the radio-tuner-next case).
  • Architecture: software radio on central computers and remote tuner modules at the antenna (radio-tuner-next); NXP sells a one-chip radio, ST cheaper tuners.

For the decision: Volume is flat; growth comes from content per car, so more of our parts per radio is the lever.

Risks

  • Grades of Si47992 and ACT8832x and the temperature range of the Si479xx tuners are not in our data.
  • Switches and oscillators are low-value sockets; qualification cost may exceed the gain at low attach (est).
  • Qorvo grades come from the “Q” suffix and the store listing; qorvo.com was not requested.
  • Several C parts are older families; long-term supply must be confirmed before a car design-in.

For the decision: Confirm the missing grades and the die question with the BUs before any qualification money is spent.
