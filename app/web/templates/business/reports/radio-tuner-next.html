Next car-radio tuner and demodulator products (2–3 years, reuse only)

20 Oct 2026 · Draft, not reviewed · BizDev broadcast data (Si479xx tuners, Si46xx coprocessors and receivers) and about 40 public web sources · “est” = estimate. Facts that rest only on search summaries are marked “(snippet)”.

In brief

  1. The radio is splitting in two. Demodulation is moving into software on the cockpit processor or into remote tuner modules (RTMs) near the antenna. The tuner becomes an RF-to-digital front end. ST already sells one (STA709/710 with JESD204B or LVDS output). NXP answers with one large chip that does everything (SAF9000: 5 tuners, all standards; preproduction).
  2. Our best 2–3-year move: an SDR-ready digital-output tuner family. It reuses the Si479xx RF cores, the dual-tuner die, phase diversity and AMIC. The audio DSP and analog demodulators come out, and a standard digital I/Q link goes in. It suits cockpit SDR (Visteon/Inntot, Renesas, Qualcomm-class SoCs) and RTMs alike.
  3. Around it, three quick moves:
    • AMIC on every tuner and as a software licence for SoC radios (the US AM-in-every-vehicle bill, EV noise);
    • an RTM reference platform combining the new tuner and the Si469xx;
    • production-ready CDR and DRM for China and India, where regulation is pulling.
    We would not try to copy NXP's all-in-one radio + audio + AI chip in this time frame.

1. Adjacent products and technologies: what we can build from what we have

Reusable blocks already in silicon or firmware:

  • wideband AM/FM/LW/SW/WB front end with loop-through;
  • Band III and L-band DAB front ends;
  • the dual tuner on one die, and multi-IC scaling to 4 tuners;
  • FM phase diversity, MRC for HD/DAB/DRM+/CDR, AMIC, DAB noise canceller and digital AGC;
  • the multistandard demod/decoder IP (HD, DAB, DRM, CDR, seamless linking);
  • automotive qualification and the HD Radio certification already in place.

Product ideas, strongest first

  1. SDR-ready digital-output tuner family (“Si479xx-D”). Two variants, both I/Q-only:
    • a dual AM/FM/DAB tuner (Band III + L-band, with PD);
    • a quad tuner built as two dual cores on one die or in one package, giving FM PD plus a background tuner plus DAB MRC.
    It has no audio DSP and no analog demodulator, and adds a standard digital I/Q output (LVDS/I2S-style or JESD204B-class). It keeps AGC, digital AGC and the DAB noise canceller in the tuner. Phase diversity can run on the chip or be exported.

    reuse RF cores, dual die, ZIF I/Q path and IQMUX exist. New work: the output interface, a larger die or a second die in the package, firmware. This is the closest answer to ST STA710 and the cheaper alternative to NXP SAF9000 for platforms that already run SDR software. Precedent: our Si47961/62 fed Renesas R-Car SDR in 2017 (snippet).

  2. AMIC everywhere, plus AMIC as SoC software. Enable AMIC on the single-tuner families (Si4790x/91x/92x) and offer it as a licence for cockpit SDR stacks working on our I/Q.

    have it firmware and licensing only. Drivers: the AM Radio for Every Vehicle Act passed the House on 15 Sep 2026 (Senate pending); several OEMs dropped AM in EVs because of noise; NXP now markets EVAM-lite. Our brochure already says AMIC is “compatible with SDR, cockpit and RTM systems”.

  3. Remote tuner module (RTM) reference platform. The new digital tuner plus the Si469xx coprocessor in the antenna module, sending audio and data to the head unit over A2B or automotive Ethernet. Ship it as a reference design with Tier-1 antenna-module makers.

    have the parts · the link PHY is a partner part. Fits zonal architectures (fewer coax runs). Hirschmann/TE already sell RTMs in EU/US/Global variants; Skyworks' own 2023 Si469xx release names RTMs over A2B/Ethernet (snippet).

  4. Regional digital radio in production: China (CDR + DRM) and India (DRM). Move Si46933/Si46943 (CDR, 3–4 channels) from Preview to production. Offer a two-chip-in-one-package tuner + coprocessor for cost-sensitive head units.

    near · China is drafting a mandatory in-car receiver standard (DRM on AM, CDR on FM; 22-month project from Sep 2024). In India about 30 % of new cars already have DRM, and the FM-band digital standard is undecided (TRAI, Oct 2025). Chinese SDR software vendors (Newglee) are moving in.

  5. Lower-cost single-standard receiver for the EU entry segment. An automotive-qualified Si468x-class single chip (tuner + DAB/DAB+ decode + DAC) for entry radios that must meet the EU digital-radio requirement.

    near Si468x exists, but is not flagged automotive. Lower priority: DAB+ is already in “almost all” new EU cars, so this is a cost play.

Not recommended in 2–3 years

  • An NXP-style radio + audio + AI SoC (5 tuners, HiFi 5, NPU, PCIe/Ethernet). It needs a new DSP and NPU, a larger process and audio software. That is new technology, beyond the time frame.
  • A new standalone audio DSP. The HiFi2-EP is dated, but audio is moving into the cockpit processor too.

2. Where else these products fit

  • Cockpit SDR platforms: Visteon (owns Inntot; more than 2 M SDR deployments by Feb 2026), Renesas R-Car, and Qualcomm-class cockpits that today still pair with external NXP radio chips.
  • Remote tuner modules and roof antenna modules: antenna makers (Hirschmann/TE and others) and Tier-1s moving to zonal architectures.
  • Two-wheelers, commercial vehicles and aftermarket in India and China, where DRM and CDR are being pushed.
  • Consumer and professional receivers: DAB+/DRM home radios and monitoring receivers can reuse the digital-output tuner (low priority).

3. Market trend

  • Software-defined radio: the demod is moving into software. Inntot doubled to more than 2 M deployments in a year, and WorldDAB describes a “shift from chip-based radio decoders to software-defined architectures”. ST already leaves demod, PD and MRC to the host.
  • Consolidation: NXP SAF9000 (5 tuners, all standards; preproduction, NDA) and SAF9800 (AM/FM + HiFi 5 + AI, Jul 2026).
  • Europe: digital radio has been mandatory in new car radios since Dec 2020, DAB+ is in almost all new cars, and 166.6 M DAB receivers have been sold. FM is lasting longer than planned: Switzerland reversed its FM switch-off, and the UK decides in autumn 2026.
  • US: HD Radio is in 125 M vehicles and over 60 % of new vehicles. The AM-in-every-vehicle bill passed the House on 15 Sep 2026. Hybrid radio (DTS AutoStage) is in 14 M vehicles.
  • India and China: regulators are choosing digital standards (DRM, CDR). These are the growth regions for new receiver silicon.
  • Supply base: thin. Only NXP and ST make tuners, while the new entrants are software-only. A strong RF-to-digital tuner keeps us relevant even when the demod moves to software.

Risks

  • Losing demod content: moving to SDR can shrink Si469xx sales. The AMIC and MRC licences, the RTM platform and the regional coprocessors offset this.
  • Interface choice: JESD204B vs LVDS/I2S vs A2B/Ethernet. Ask Visteon, Renesas and Qualcomm-platform Tier-1s which one before choosing.
  • NXP SAF9000: if it reaches volume at a good price, the stand-alone tuner market narrows to platforms that don't buy NXP.
  • Regulation timing: the US AM bill, the China standard and the India standard decision are all pending.
  • Evidence gaps: nxp.com, st.com and investors.skyworksinc.com blocked automated access, so several competitor facts are from search summaries.
