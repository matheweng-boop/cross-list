What it is

Component certifications: UL 1577 (isolation rating), VDE / IEC 60747-17 (working voltage, transients, surge, basic or reinforced), CSA component acceptance, CQC (China) and sometimes TÜV to end-product standards.

When it deviates

A missing certification can mean extra end-product testing or a market you cannot ship to. It does not change how the part works, so it is information (priority 2) here; your compliance plan decides whether it matters.
