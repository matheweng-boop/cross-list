What it is

Creepage is the shortest path along the package surface between the two sides of the barrier; clearance is the shortest path through air. Wide-body SOIC packages offer about 8 mm, extra-wide packages 14.5 mm or more.

When it deviates

The minimum creepage follows from the working voltage, the pollution degree and the material group (CTI) of the mold compound, per IEC 60664-1 and the end-product standard; reinforced insulation needs twice the basic value. Less package creepage can make the part unusable at your voltage even if the electrical ratings match.

How to judge it

Look up the required creepage for your working voltage, pollution degree and material group in your standard, and check the PCB keeps it too (slots or keep-outs under the package). A standards-based calculator needs those inputs and is not modelled here.

Not modelled Creepage tables differ by standard; we do not guess them.
