What it is

Some isolators also carry power across the barrier with an integrated DC/DC converter, so the isolated side needs no separate supply.

Why it is never a deviation

The board around a part with integrated power is different (no isolated supply, different pins and decoupling), so it defines the function. If you compare efficiency, the heat it dissipates is Pout·(1/η − 1): at 3 W, 70 % efficiency dissipates about 1.29 W, 85 % about 0.53 W.
