What it is

Basic insulation gives one level of protection against electric shock. Reinforced insulation is a single insulation system that counts as double insulation: it may be the only barrier between hazardous voltage and something a person can touch. Reinforced parts are certified to stricter surge, test and lifetime requirements (IEC 60747-17).

When it deviates

A basic part cannot replace a reinforced one where the isolator is the protective barrier: the system would need a second layer of insulation. A reinforced part can always replace a basic one.

How to judge it

Ask what the barrier protects. Functional isolation (noise, ground loops) needs neither class; protection of accessible circuits from mains or high-voltage sides usually needs reinforced. Your safety concept decides; the system standard sets the rules.
