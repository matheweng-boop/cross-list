What it is

VISO is the rms voltage the barrier withstands for 60 seconds in qualification (UL 1577); production parts are tested for about one second at a higher level. It is a withstand rating: it shows how robust the barrier is, not what it can carry continuously. That is the working voltage.

When it deviates

The end-product standard (for example IEC 62368-1, IEC 61800-5-1 or IEC 60601-1) sets the test voltage the insulation must pass. A part rated below that cannot be used, even if everything else matches. A higher rating than needed gives no functional benefit, only robustness margin.

How to judge it

Compare both ratings with the test voltage your standard requires for your working voltage and insulation class. If the candidate is lower but still above the requirement, the deviation is acceptable; document it.
