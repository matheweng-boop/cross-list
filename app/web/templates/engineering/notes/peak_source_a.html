What it is

The peak current the driver can source (turn-on) and sink (turn-off) into the gate. Together with the gate charge it sets how fast the power device switches.

When it deviates

A weaker driver moves the gate charge more slowly: slower edges, more switching loss, less CMTI stress. A stronger one can be slowed down with a gate resistor; a weaker one cannot be made faster.

Quick check Engineering estimate

t ≈ Qg/I at constant peak current: a lower bound, not the voltage transition time. The real current is limited by the driver's output resistance and the gate resistor.
