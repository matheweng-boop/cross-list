What it is

AEC-Q100 is the automotive stress-test qualification for ICs. Automotive grades also come with production part approval (PPAP), change notification and traceability that automotive customers require. At Skyworks this is the -AS / -AU suffix (industrial: -IS / -IU); TI and Novosense use -Q1.

When it deviates

An industrial part cannot replace an automotive part in an automotive design, even if every electrical value matches. An automotive part can replace an industrial one; it may cost more.

How BizDev treats it

Automotive grade is a hard requirement when the reference is automotive: industrial parts are not listed at all, not even as a package equivalent. For an industrial reference, AEC-Q100 parts are listed as an upgrade, and the results can be filtered by grade. Every part carries an AEC-Q100 or Industrial badge.
