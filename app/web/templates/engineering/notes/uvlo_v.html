What it is

The output-side undervoltage lockout: below this supply voltage the driver holds the gate off.

Why variants are not interchangeable

The threshold must suit the power device. A lower UVLO than intended lets a SiC MOSFET or IGBT run with too little gate voltage (high on-resistance, overheating); a higher one can shut down a correctly supplied gate drive. Treat a different UVLO as a functional difference, not a better or worse value.

When no variant matches

Vendors offer different steps (Skyworks 4 / 8 / 12 / 15 V, Novosense 6 / 8 / 13 V, Infineon 9.3 / 12 / 13.6 V). When no variant matches, the cross ranks the next lower threshold first (owner, 22 Oct 2026): it still starts up at every supply the original starts at, while a higher one may keep the driver off. Example: Novosense NSI6602A (6 V option) → Si82E29AGC (4 V), not Si82E29ABC (8 V). It stays a deviation: the lower part protects the power device less at low supply, so check the gate supply in the design.
