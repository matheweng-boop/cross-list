What it is

VIORM is the maximum repetitive peak voltage the barrier may see for its rated lifetime (IEC 60747-17, formerly VDE 0884-11). Its companions are VIOWM (the same limit as rms or DC), VIOTM (short transients) and VIOSM (surge). The isolation rating (VISO, a one-minute test) says nothing about lifetime; the working voltage does.

When it deviates

A lower working voltage shortens the margin between your continuous barrier stress (DC link, or the peak of an AC bus) and what the part is qualified for. If your stress exceeds it, the part is not qualified for that use at all.
