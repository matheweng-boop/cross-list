Interactive visuals

Drag the sliders and watch the physics: each visual shows the equation, the same equation with your numbers, a live graph and what to notice. Reference lines (isolator CMTI, package creepage, driver current) come from the cross lists. The RF bands chart shows every cellular, GNSS, Wi-Fi, satellite and radar band with the parts that cover it. Built from the standard equations; for background reading see OpenStax University Physics ↗ (Rice University, free textbooks). Each visual names the product families it explains and links to the physics behind our products. More to try, free in the browser: Falstad circuit simulator ↗, PhET ↗, Wolfram Demonstrations ↗ and Desmos ↗ (what each is good for: Library sources).
