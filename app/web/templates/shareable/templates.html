How to use it

  1. Open it in PowerPoint, click any “Your …” text and type. Delete the slides you do not need.
  2. Morph slides come in pairs or runs: duplicate them together and keep the names that start with !! (Home › Arrange › Selection Pane).
  3. Change all colours at once: Design › Variants › Colors (Windows and Mac). On an iPad, phone or the web, download a colour version above instead.
  4. Pictures: right-click › Change Picture on both zoom slides. Charts: right-click › Edit Data.
  5. Play it as a slide show to see the movement; the last two slides repeat these tips.
  6. Each report under Reports also has Animated .pptx: the report itself, laid out with this look.
